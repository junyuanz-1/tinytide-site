Support

Answers to common questions about Lullo.

How do I share with my partner or a caregiver?
  1. Open More → Family & Caregivers and tap Share [baby's name].
  2. Send the invitation by Messages, Mail or any other app.
  3. They open the link on an iPhone with Lullo installed and accept. The baby appears in their app and stays in sync.

Both of you need to be signed in to iCloud. From the same screen you can see who has access, change permissions, or stop sharing.

I got a new iPhone. How do I move my data?

Same Apple ID: sign in to iCloud on the new phone and install Lullo. Your data downloads automatically.

Different Apple ID: on the old phone, go to More → Backup & Transfer → Export Backup File, AirDrop it to the new phone, then choose Import Backup File there. Importing merges entries and never creates duplicates.

My entries are not syncing between devices.
  • Check that you are signed in to iCloud on every device, with iCloud enabled for Lullo (Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Apps Using iCloud).
  • If your iCloud storage is full, sync pauses until there is space. Your data is still safe on the device.
  • Open Lullo and keep it open for a minute. More → iCloud Sync shows the last sync time.
What do I get with Lullo Supporter?

Every tracking feature in Lullo is free. Supporters get alternate app icons, theme colors and a supporter badge as a thank-you, and the subscription can be shared with your family through Family Sharing.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Lullo on your iPhone, or tap Manage on the Lullo Supporter screen in the app. Apple handles all billing.

I reinstalled the app. How do I restore my subscription?

Go to More → Support Lullo → Lullo Supporter and tap Restore Purchases.

Is Lullo medical advice?

No. Sleep predictions, growth percentiles and health alerts are general guidance only. Always talk to your pediatrician about your baby's health.

Still need help?

Email us at lullo.support@gmail.com and we'll get back to you, usually within a few days. Please tell us your iPhone model and iOS version, and don't include your baby's personal details.

帮助与支持

关于摇摇的常见问题。

怎么和伴侣或看护人共享？
  1. 打开 更多 → 家人与看护人，点 共享[宝宝名字]。
  2. 通过信息、邮件或任意 App 发送邀请。
  3. 对方在装有摇摇的 iPhone 上打开链接并接受，宝宝就会出现在对方的 App 里，之后自动同步。

双方都需要登录 iCloud。在同一页面可以查看成员、修改权限或停止共享。

换了新 iPhone，数据怎么迁移？

同一个 Apple ID：在新手机上登录 iCloud 并安装摇摇，数据会自动下载。

不同的 Apple ID：在旧手机上进入 更多 → 备份与迁移 → 导出备份文件，用隔空投送发到新手机，再在新手机上选择 导入备份文件。导入会自动合并，不会产生重复记录。

记录在不同设备之间没有同步？
  • 确认每台设备都登录了 iCloud，并且允许摇摇使用 iCloud（设置 → [你的名字] → iCloud → 使用 iCloud 的 App）。
  • 如果 iCloud 空间已满，同步会暂停，直到有可用空间；数据仍然安全地保存在手机上。
  • 打开摇摇并停留一分钟。更多 → iCloud 同步 中可以看到上次同步时间。
“摇摇支持者”有什么权益？

摇摇的所有记录功能都是免费的。作为感谢，支持者可以使用替换图标、主题配色和支持者徽章，并且可以通过家人共享和家人一起享有。

怎么取消或更改订阅？

在 iPhone 上打开 设置 → [你的名字] → 订阅 → 摇摇，或者在 App 的“摇摇支持者”页面点 管理。所有扣费都由 Apple 处理。

重装了 App，怎么恢复订阅？

进入 更多 → 支持摇摇 → 摇摇支持者，点 恢复购买。

摇摇提供医疗建议吗？

不提供。睡眠预测、生长百分位和健康提示仅供参考。宝宝的健康问题请咨询儿科医生。

还需要帮助？

请发邮件到 lullo.support@gmail.com，我们通常会在几天内回复。请告诉我们你的 iPhone 型号和 iOS 版本，不要在邮件中填写宝宝的个人信息。
